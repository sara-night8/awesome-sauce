# awesome-sauce
The operating system for stronger masjids.

# The Problem
Masjids are managed by dedicated volunteers, however these volunteers are parents, doctors, engineers and more. They are responsible for running the business side of the masjid like: grants, governance, deadlines, and finances all without formal education about how to do this 

# The Solution
MasjidOps centralizes these responsibilities and gives leaders personalized recommendations based on their masjid’s profile.

Grants are the main demo feature, but the full vision includes:

- Operations
- Governance
- Grant funding
- Documents and policies
- Tasks and deadlines
- Institutional knowledge

# Target Users
Volunteer Executive Board Members at small to mid-sized U.S. masjids (annual budgets of roughly $100,000–$2 million) who are responsible for running the business side of the mosque without formal nonprofit management experience.

# How Automatic Grant-Matching Works
Users do not need to manually search.

After onboarding, MasjidOps uses information such as:

- State and county
- Organization type
- Programs
- Funding needs
- Annual budget
- Facility ownership
- 501(c)(3) status
- SAM.gov registration
- UEI status
- Existing documents
- Grant history
- Deadline readiness

The system then recommends relevant opportunities and explains:

- Why the grant may match
- What requirements are satisfied
- What information is missing
- What needs to be verified
- What the masjid should do next
